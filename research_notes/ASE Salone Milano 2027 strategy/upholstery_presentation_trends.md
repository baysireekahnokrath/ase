## 1. Upholstery form trends (soft architecture, modular, low lounge, tailoring, bouclé)

### Takeaway
From 2024 to 2026 the direction moved from "swollen", puffy, oversized foam forms (2024–25) towards soft volumes that have structure and detailed finishing. In 2026 the headline pieces were quilted, duvet-like modules with visible stitching (Cassina Ardys), deep modular systems drawn from architecture (Minotti Orion, Poltrona Frau Blisscape Deep), and soft seating set against hard lacquer. Bouclé's dominance has clearly faded. Mohair/velvet pile, shearling, corduroy, tweedy wools and leather inserts are taking its place.

### Cited Findings
**2024**
- Oversized foam sofas became centre pieces at Salone 2024. COR, B&B Italia and Muuto showed "huge foam sofas" — [architare](https://www.architare.de/en/blog/spannende-trends-aus-mailand-salone-del-mobile-2024)
- Wallpaper* 2024 described "a diffused sense of comfort", with upholstery moving towards puffier, squishier forms — [Wallpaper*](https://www.wallpaper.com/design-interiors/interior-design-trends-2024-salone-del-mobile)
- Extra-large pieces got even more generous dimensions, with soft, rounded lines — [Houzz 2024](https://www.houzz.com/magazine/5-furniture-trends-from-salone-del-mobile-2024-stsetivw-vs~175105596). Furnishings were increasingly modular — [Sediarreda](https://www.sediarreda.com/en/magazine/salone-del-mobile-2024)
- Bouclé "seemed less widespread" at Salone 2024. Long-pile fur covers appeared on armchairs, poufs and cushions. This is a single-source observation — [architare](https://www.architare.de/en/blog/spannende-trends-aus-mailand-salone-del-mobile-2024)

**2025**

### Inferences
- **[INFERENCE]** For April 2027, the strongest form story is **"soft architecture with tailoring"**: generous, low, deep modules whose softness is made precise through quilting lines, visible top-stitching, channels, welts and wrapped edges, often set against a hard surface (lacquer, stone, wood). This matches ASE's "cut, sew, pad, wrap, form" capability and its calm, architectural mood better than the inflated 2024–25 look, which editors now treat as past.
- **[INFERENCE]** Visible craft details (stitch lines, pleats, piping) are now a point of differentiation. Showing them in close-up on the booth (cut sections, half-finished frames) would make upholstery skill legible to visitors.
- **[INFERENCE]** With hospitality and contract rising (WWD's "hotel-native" theme and the reported 2027 Salon Contract), ASE PROJECTS should show modular lounge and banquette systems with replaceable covers and contract-grade specifications.
- **[INFERENCE]** Avoid leading with bouclé. Mohair/velvet pile, tweedy wool, corduroy-scale ribs and leather are safer "tactile" signals for 2027.

## 2. Material trends (leather, tactile textiles, foam alternatives, demountable construction)

### Takeaway
Leather had a clear 2025–2026 moment in Milan. It was led by fashion and luxury houses (Hermès, Louis Vuitton, Bottega Veneta woven leather, Longchamp) and by saddlery and vegetable-tanned furniture from Uniqka (with Lara Bohinc and Studiopepe), and it reached mainstream sofas as leather straps, belts and inserts. On the materials-science side, the strongest signals were polyurethane-foam alternatives (Vitra's recyclable V-Foam in 2025; a disassemblable, glue-free, low-foam Vitra sofa; Dezeen's "foamless sofas" roundup using latex, wool, coir, pocket springs and 3D knits). Mono-material and repairable claims remain thin.

## 3. Colour directions for 2026–2027

### Takeaway
At Milan 2026, colour was warm and earthy: chocolate, tobacco and cognac browns (often in leather), terracotta, plum, red and burgundy, plus olive. Lacquer appeared in softened shades (warm brown, mint, olive, pale blue). The forecasters split: Pantone 2026 chose a white (Cloud Dancer), Heimtextil 26/27 chose earthy neutrals (sand, clay, soot, olive, bark plus Burgundy Patina), Heimtextil 27/28 chose rust, ochre, burnt orange and dusty pink against technical blues and greens, and WGSN/Coloro chose Luminous Blue for 2027.

## 4. Notable upholstery launches and what made them stand out (Salone 2024–2026 and Fuorisalone/Alcova)

### Takeaway
The launches that drew attention combined a recognisable designer, a clear material idea and a narrative: Cassina Ardys (stitching as texture), Minotti Orion (architecture as concept), Poltrona Frau Blisscape Deep (comfort depth), Tacchini Butter (playful block volumes), Vitra V-Foam (a solution to a material problem), and fashion-house leather objects (craft heritage). In Alcova and other emerging-maker coverage, material experiments (recycled mattress covers, foam-free fillings, deconstruction) were the hook.

## 5. Presentation trends and booth practicalities

### Takeaway
The most press-covered presentations in 2025–2026 were immersive, architectural or multisensory installations (Lina Ghotmeh's labyrinth, the USM × Snøhetta "Renaissance of the Real", Zaha Hadid Architects' "The Origin", Bocci light ceilings, the SolidNature/OMA "Il Sonno", the Marimekko × Laila Gohar bedroom installation) and fashion-house home installations that tell a craft-heritage story (Hermès, Bottega Veneta). Live upholstery demonstrations were rarely documented. That leaves a gap ASE could fill. The trade-fair context for buyers is large and international (316,342 visitors, 68% of trade visitors international).

### Inferences
- **[INFERENCE]** Press attention goes to presentations that are **spatial, sensory and story-led** (a room, a labyrinth, a material world) rather than product rows. For ASE, an "atelier" staging — a calm architectural room in ASE colours where visitors touch leather, wool and foam layers and see a live cut/sew/wrap station — would combine the touch-led and maker-story formats. The search found no prominent precedent for live upholstery making, which suggests a gap ASE could own.
- **[INFERENCE]** A material library (swatch wall of leathers, textiles, foam/latex cross-sections, REFORM offcut panels) would serve both press (visual, tactile) and buyers (specification). A bedroom or hotel-room vignette would tie into Moroso's "Me Time" bedroom theme, the "hotel-native" trend and the 2027 Salon Contract.
- **[INFERENCE]** Buyer-meeting practicalities: with 68% of trade visitors international and the fair spread over six days (13–18 April 2027), plan pre-booked meeting slots, a quiet meeting zone off the experiential space, spec sheets and QR swatch kits, and trade-only days for PROJECTS conversations. This is a general practice inference; no source in this research measured which booth features drive meetings.
