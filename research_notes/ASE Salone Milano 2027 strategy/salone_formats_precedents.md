# Salone del Mobile.Milano 2027 and the Milan Design Week landscape: formats, costs, deadlines and Asian/Thai precedents (research notes for ASE)

Research date: 9 Nov 2026. Method note: the session's network blocked direct fetching of salonemilano.it, lb.salonemilano.it, dezeen.com, designwanted.com, khaosodenglish.com, archiproducts.com and canadianinteriors.com. Findings below come from search-engine summaries of those pages, not full-page reads. Every figure needs checking against the primary page before anyone relies on it in a decision document. Each item is labelled **[CONFIRMED 2027]**, **[HISTORICAL]** (2024 to 2026 fact) or **[INFERENCE]**.

## 1. Dates, rotation (Euroluce year), Salone Contract, and visitor/exhibitor numbers

### Takeaway
Salone del Mobile.Milano 2027 (65th edition) is confirmed for **13 to 18 April 2027** at Fiera Milano Rho. Fuorisalone runs **12 to 18 April 2027** under the theme "Design In Relation". 2027 is a **Euroluce year**, so EuroCucina and the Bathroom exhibition sit out. 2027 also launches **Salone Contract**, a new platform for hotels, offices, residences and retail with a masterplan by OMA. That is the most relevant new fact for an upholstery and contract maker like ASE. Attendance was 370,824 in 2024 (a kitchen/bath year), about 302,500 in 2025 (a Euroluce year) and 316,342 in 2026.

### Inferences
- **[INFERENCE]** Euroluce takes up several halls in odd years. Furniture exhibitors (including upholstery) therefore compete for the remaining hall space, and Salone Contract adds a further demand on halls. Expect total footprint and exhibitor counts close to 2025 levels (around 2,000 exhibitors, around 300k visitors). Odd-year visitor numbers have been lower than even-year (kitchen/bath) numbers: 2025's 302.5k versus 2024's 370.8k.
- **[INFERENCE]** Euroluce years draw lighting specifiers, architects and interior designers. Combined with Salone Contract, the 2027 visitor mix should lean further toward contract, hospitality and architecture professionals. That suits ASE's OEM and project capability (ASE PROJECTS) better than a pure retail-buyer audience would.
- **[INFERENCE]** Salone Contract's stated rationale is a market in which value moves "from single products to the ability to combine products, services and management". That matches ASE's "co-creation partner, not supplier" positioning almost word for word. It is the strongest strategic hook for 2027.

- Exact exhibitor counts by sector (upholstery) and by country (Thailand) are not available.

## 2. Main fair (Rho) admission: process, costs, deadlines, realism for a first-time Asian brand

### Takeaway
New exhibitors submit an Application Form and are then contacted by the Commercial Office. Admission is at the organiser's discretion, and space for established exhibitors is allocated far ahead. The official space-only price found was **€207/m² + VAT** (four open sides), plus a **€15/m² first-timer supplement**. That figure comes from a page still showing 2025 dates. Stand build typically adds €300 to €950+/m² at Fiera Milano, possibly 20 to 30% more at Salone (vendor estimate). For November 2026, a solo Rho stand for April 2027 is unrealistic for a newcomer. The Thailand Pavilion (DITP) is the realistic route into Rho.

### Cited Findings
- **[HISTORICAL, likely still valid]** "Companies interested in exhibiting for the first time or wishing to participate after an absence of one edition" fill in the Application Form and are "subsequently contacted by the Commercial Office". — [Salone: How to participate](https://www.salonemilano.it/en/how-participate)
- **[HISTORICAL, possibly outdated]** The base price is €207.00/m² + VAT for a four-open-sides stand, with a €15.00/m² + VAT supplement for first-time applicants or companies absent from the last two editions. The page showed 2025 dates. — [Salone: How to participate](https://www.salonemilano.it/en/how-participate)
- **[UNVERIFIED]** A third-party agent (Jufair) lists a raw space price of €310/m² for 13 to 18 April 2027. This is unofficial and may include agent mark-up. — [jufair.com](https://en.jufair.com/exhibition/salone-milano/apply)
- **[HISTORICAL]** Exhibitors must comply with the Fiera Milano Technical Regulations and the Salone "Regulations for the Design of Stands". There is a set-up project submission process. Sustainability guidelines include a ban on plasterboard walls, infill and false ceilings (Fiera Milano tech regs version 3-2025). — [Salone: Technical regulations & sustainable set-up](https://www.salonemilano.it/en/technical-regulations-and-sustainable-set); [Submission of set-up project](https://www.salonemilano.it/en/submission-set-project); [SMM 2026 sustainable installation guidelines (PDF)](https://www.salonemilano.it/sites/default/files/2025-07-14/SMM_2026_GUIDELINES%20FOR%20SUSTAINABLE%20TRADE%20FAIR%20INSTALLATIONS_0.pdf); [SdM 2024 Regulations for design of stands (PDF)](https://www.salonemilano.it/sites/default/files/2023-07-31/SdM%202024_Regulations%20for%20the%20design%20of%20stands.pdf)
- **[VENDOR ESTIMATE]** A built stand at Fiera Milano typically costs about €300 to €950+/m² depending on size and finish, with Salone projects 20 to 30% above that. The source is a stand builder with a commercial interest. — [Adam Expo Stand](https://adamexpostand.com/exhibition-stand-builder-fiera-milano/)
- **[HISTORICAL]** The foreign exhibitor share was 45% in 2025 and 36.6% in 2026. The fair is dominated by Italian manufacturers. — sources as in section 1

### Inferences
- **[INFERENCE, illustrative arithmetic, not a quote]** A 48 m² solo stand would cost roughly:
  - Space: 48 × (€207 + €15) ≈ €10.7k
  - Build: 48 × €400 to €1,200 ≈ €19k to €58k
  - Services, power, insurance, shipping, travel and staffing: likely another €25k to €60k

  That gives roughly €55k to €130k all-in. Verify against an official 2027 price list.
- **[INFERENCE]** Halls are booked almost a year ahead, and the call for established exhibitors closes long before November. A newcomer applying in November 2026 would at best go on a waiting list for April 2027. The Thailand Pavilion (DITP), the new Salone Contract platform, or an off-site Fuorisalone show are the realistic 2027 options. A solo Rho stand is better treated as a 2028 or 2029 goal, after building press and buyer recognition.
- **[INFERENCE]** The Salone curates for brand and design quality, not only payment. An OEM with no own-brand design track record would struggle in a direct application. A designer collaboration and a distinct collection would strengthen any application.

### Gaps
- No official 2027 deadline, waiting-list policy or selection-committee criteria were found. salonemilano.it could not be fetched, and search snippets do not show them. Action: email the Commercial Office directly.
- No official 2027 price list was found, and the €207/m² figure needs confirming.

## 3. SaloneSatellite 2027

### Takeaway
SaloneSatellite is for **designers under 35**. Applications for 2027 **closed on 30 August 2026**, and only a waiting list remains. It is not a route for a manufacturer brand, although ASE could produce prototypes for a young selected designer.

### Cited Findings
- **[CONFIRMED 2027]** Applications for SaloneSatellite 2027 were open until 30 August 2026. A later official page states that applications are closed and refers to a waiting list. — [Salone: How to take part SaloneSatellite 2027](https://www.salonemilano.it/en/articles/how-take-part-salonesatellite-2027); [Salone SaloneSatellite exhibition page](https://www.salonemilano.it/en/exhibitions/salonesatellite); [WBDM Belgium](https://www.wbdm.be/en/news/salonesatellite-2027/); [immobiliare.it](https://www.immobiliare.it/news/progettare-casa/architettura-e-design/salonesatellite-sono-aperte-le-candidature-397479/)
- **[CONFIRMED]** Eligibility: designers under 35 (freelancers, recent graduates, early-career). Applicants submit three projects in English, each with 1 to 3 photos and a description of up to 300 characters. — [Salone SaloneSatellite 2027 article](https://www.salonemilano.it/en/articles/how-take-part-salonesatellite-2027); [Salone 2026 call](https://www.salonemilano.it/en/articles/salonesatellite-2026-how-to-take-part)
- **[HISTORICAL]** Recent themes: "Connecting Design since 1998" (2025) and a 2026 call around reinterpreting ancient techniques sustainably. No 2027 theme was found in the sources. — [Salone SaloneSatellite 2025](https://www.salonemilano.it/en/articles/salonesatellite-2025-applications-are-open); [stone-ideas 2026](https://www.stone-ideas.com/114518/applications-open-for-salone-satellite-2026/)

### Inferences
- **[INFERENCE]** Companies do not enter SaloneSatellite as exhibitors. A legitimate indirect play is to sponsor or produce prototypes for a young Thai designer accepted into the show, credited as "made by ASE". That would be a co-creation story. The 2027 call has already closed, so this is a 2028 option, with a deadline likely around mid-2027 by pattern.

### Gaps
- The 2027 SaloneSatellite theme and the Thai designers selected for 2027 could not be confirmed.
- No rule on maker or producer credit was found.

## 4. Fuorisalone options (Alcova, districts, platforms, private shows)

### Takeaway
Fuorisalone is open-access and press-heavy. Alcova (131 exhibitors in 2026, at the Baggio Military Hospital and Villa Pestarini) is the most press-dense curated off-site platform, but its fees and application process are not published. Budgets found: about **€30k to €50k** for a modest installation in a shared space, and **€100k to €250k** for a mid-range brand activation. Milan event space costs €450 to €800/day normally and much more during design week. No official district fees for Brera, 5VIE or Tortona were found.

### Cited Findings
- **[HISTORICAL]** Alcova 2026 (11th edition, 20 to 26 April) took place at the Baggio Military Hospital complex and Franco Albini's Villa Pestarini, with 131 exhibitors. It is founded by Valentina Ciuffi (Studio Vedèt) and Joseph Grima (Space Caviar). In 2025 a €25 visitor ticket was introduced for one Alcova venue. — [alcova.xyz Milano 2026](https://www.alcova.xyz/exhibitions/milano-2026); [myartguides](https://myartguides.com/events/milan/alcova-2026/); [fuorisalone.it Alcova 2026 feature](https://www.fuorisalone.it/en/magazine/focus/article/1922/places-names-and-projects-featured-in-alcova-2026-milano-fuorisalone); [Domus 2025 ticket](https://www.domusweb.it/it/notizie/2025/03/18/alcova-biglietto-fuorisalone-2025.html)
- **[UNVERIFIED / secondary]** Budget ranges: a modest installation in a shared space costs about €30k to €50k, and a mid-range brand activation about €100k to €250k. Normal event space is €450 to €800/day or about €2.4k to €4k/week, with design week "considerably higher". These come from a venue-broker guide that may be a year old. — [Milan Venue Fuorisalone guide](https://www.milanvenue.com/blog/italy-events/fuorisalone-milan-plan-attendance.html); [The Storefront](https://www.thestorefront.com/search/milan/event)
- **[HISTORICAL]** Brera Design Week runs its own events guide and Fuorisalone passport. Space listings exist for Tortona (150 m²) and Brera (205 m²) for design week, without public prices. — [Brera Design Week 2026](https://breradesignweek.it/2026/en/eventsfuorisalone-passport); [Milan Venue showroom spaces](https://www.milanvenue.com/design-week-fuorisalone.html)
- **[HISTORICAL]** Thai precedent in Ventura Lambrate (a "Ventura-type" platform): Thinkk Studio showed there with Studio 248 in 2012, and Slow Hand Design/DITP staged "Bar Crops and Props" there in 2016. — [Dezeen 2012](https://www.dezeen.com/2012/03/28/furniture-by-thinkk-studio-and-studio-248-at-ventura-lambrate/amp/); [Fuorisalone archive 2016](https://archivio.fuorisalone.it/2016/en/events/1150/Bar-Crops-and-Props)

### Inferences
- **[INFERENCE]** Audience split:
  - Rho is buyer and contract heavy: 68% international trade visitors (official figure).
  - Alcova, Brera and Isola draw mostly press, curators, architects and the design community.
  - Tortona, Superstudio and Base Milano mix brand activations with the general public.

  For a brand repositioning from OEM to "A Space Elevated", an off-site Fuorisalone show yields press and imagery (brand equity). Rho and Salone Contract yield project leads.
- **[INFERENCE]** Curated platforms such as Alcova typically select between autumn and winter for April. By November 2026 ASE would need to approach immediately with a strong concept. A rented apartment or showroom in Brera or 5VIE, paired with an appointment-based contract programme, is the most controllable fallback.

### Gaps
- No published Alcova exhibitor fees, application window or criteria were found. alcova.xyz is JavaScript-only, and the fee is likely quoted privately.
- No official fees were found for Brera Design District, 5VIE, Isola, Tortona, Superdesign Show or Base Milano. Contact each organiser for a quote.
- No reliable press-impact metrics by platform were found.

## 5. Thai and Asian precedents

### Takeaway
Thailand has a recurring **DITP-run Thailand Pavilion inside Rho**: Hall 7, booth D18 in 2025, and a "Host & Home" section in 2026 with about 15 established furniture brands. In 2026 DITP took 25 Thai brands to Milan Design Week. This is ASE's most practical entry route for 2027. Japan's makers (Karimoku, Maruni, Ritzwell) show that sustained, design-led, multi-year presence with recognised designers works. Korea and the Philippines use state-backed curated shows.

## 6. Practical logistics (shipping, ATA Carnet, accommodation, timeline)

### Takeaway
The Board of Trade of Thailand issues ATA Carnets for temporary export of exhibition goods. Italy is on carnet country lists, but the Italy-specific conditions were not verified. Validity limits differ between sources (one year versus six months for exhibition goods). Specific Thailand-to-Milan sea freight lead times and accommodation data were not found in this round.

### Cited Findings
- **[CONFIRMED, current regime]** The Board of Trade of Thailand is approved by Customs to issue ATA Carnets and acts as guarantor. Contact: ata@thaichamber.org, +66 2018 6888 ext. 3710/3910/3020. — [thailand.go.th ATA Carnet](https://www.thailand.go.th/issue-focus-detail/001_03_148?hl=en); [TCEB MICE OSS](https://miceoss.tceb.or.th/en/logistics-goods/others/); [thailand.go.th export clearance](https://thailand.go.th/issue-focus-detail/001_03_167?hl=en)
